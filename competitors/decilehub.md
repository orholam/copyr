# Competitor Deep Dive: Decile Hub (Decile Group)

**Report date:** August 23, 2026
**Scope:** Publicly available information only. Every factual claim below is sourced; company self-reported claims are explicitly labeled as such. Analysis sections are clearly separated from facts.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Decile Hub is the AI-powered SaaS "operating system for modern venture capital" sold by **Decile Group**, a Palo Alto-based, full-stack platform for emerging VC fund managers. Decile Group is not a pure software vendor: it runs an integrated ecosystem spanning free accelerator training (VC Lab), freemium fund-operations software (Decile Hub), outsourced fund administration (Decile Partners), a fund-in-a-box product (Start Fund), LP-side products (LP Institute, Decile Pro), and a fund of funds (Decile Capital). The stated mission is to **launch 10,000 next-generation VC firms by 2030**.

The company raised a **$14M seed round in July 2025 co-led by Draper Associates, Javelin Venture Partners, and Geek Ventures** — its first disclosed institutional round despite operating since 2021. Its distribution engine is unusual and defensible: free training converts managers into software users, software users into fund-admin customers. Third-party coverage (Axios) has flagged its AI tooling; third-party data providers benchmark it against AngelList, Edda, and Odin.

**Business-perspective verdict:** Decile Group is the volume leader in the emerging-manager segment with a full-lifecycle revenue ladder (education → SaaS → services → capital), but it is heavily dependent on (a) the health of the emerging-VC formation cycle, (b) conversion of free users into paying admin customers, and (c) self-reported traction metrics that are difficult to verify externally.

## 3. Founder & Origin Story

**Adeo Ressi** (b. 1972, NYC) is a serial entrepreneur best known as:
- Co-founder/Executive Chairman of the **Founder Institute** (launched 2009; 200+ cities; FI says 9,000+ companies launched, incl. unicorns Udemy and Esusu).
- Creator of **TheFunded** (2007), the anonymous VC-review site.
- Credited (by his own organizations) as inventor of the **Convertible Equity** agreement that preceded Y Combinator's SAFE, plus Class F common stock and the Cornerstone LPA used in fund formation.
- University of Pennsylvania roommate of Elon Musk; former X PRIZE Foundation board member.

Ressi launched **VC Lab in early 2020 under the Founder Institute**; it was folded into **Decile Group, founded in 2021**, where Ressi is CEO. The organization also authored the **Mensarius Oath**, a Hippocratic-style ethical code all VC Lab participants must endorse — a core part of brand identity ("transforming venture capital into a force for good").

Business significance: Ressi brings two decades of founder-community distribution (Founder Institute network) and personal brand credibility with exactly the buyer persona Decile targets — first-time fund managers.

### Decile Hub feature detail (from product pages)
- **Fundraising:** digital commitment pages (PACT workflow), automated LP onboarding, secure data rooms, capital call automation, LP portal & reporting, email automation.
- **Deal flow:** deal memo builder, company submission portal, portfolio monitoring, investment analytics, cap table management, automated portfolio-company updates. Five ingestion methods per their own guide: manual+AI, Chrome plugin, email parsing of forwarded decks, bulk upload, inquiry form.
- **Back office:** multi-entity (management co / GP / fund / SPV) accounting, financial reporting, tax document distribution, expense tracking.
- **Compliance:** digital signing, agreement templates, regulatory filing support, multi-jurisdiction support.
- **AI:** Venture AI Co-pilot answering legal/tax/operational questions from thousands of expert responses; AI-assisted deal memos; smart automations (e.g., LPA review/signing flows).

## 6. Pricing & Unit Economics Signals

- **Freemium at the core.** Decile's own April 7, 2026 "Venture Capital CRM Guide" states: "The core functionality is free. That's not a stripped-down trial." This undercuts Affinity (claimed "thousands of dollars per month" in the same post) and pressures every paid VC CRM.
- Third-party comparisons note **pricing is not published** beyond the free tier; costs are customized by firm size/modules/support (Fundingstack comparison page).
- Monetization therefore concentrates in: (a) Decile Partners admin contracts, (b) Start Fund economics (fee/carry participation), (c) advanced Hub capabilities, (d) Decile Capital economics.
- Claimed customer savings: consolidating onto Hub saves firms **>$65K/year vs. separate providers** (fi.co insight page).

### Engagement-to-outcome data (self-published, from 750+ monthly active users)
Published correlations on fi.co: daily users secure ~5x more LP commitments than occasional users; weekly users average $1.4M in commitments vs $0.3M for infrequent users; long-term users (180+ days) secure 45x more than new users; managers tracking 1,000+ prospects average $3.5M vs $0.2M for <25 prospects. *Caveat: correlation ≠ causation and it is marketing content — but it reveals how Decile measures success (LP dollars committed through the platform) and functions as a retention/usage playbook.*

## 7. Traction & Customers

- ~**1,000 VC firms** use Hub monthly (~750+ MAU cited in engagement study); site currently shows "1K active VC firm users," "26K startups tracked," "165K LPs tracked."
- LP commitments facilitated through platform processes: **over $1B cumulative** (older claim) now shown as **$1.5B powered**; **$110M committed in April 2024 alone** (fi.co insight).
- VC Lab cohort outcomes are showcased as case studies, e.g., Mala Ramakrishnan closed Progressive Ventures Fund I at **$12M from 94 LPs** (Aug 17, 2026 case study); Jessica Kamada is cited as a success story in Decile's Apr 2026 CRM guide.
- Customer testimonials on decilehub.com praise K-1/reporting automation speed; one testimonial explicitly frames the competitive dynamic: *"the product will continue to crush Carta and AngelList with this kind of responsive development!"*
- Target customer profile: **first-time GPs and emerging managers** — Decile's own materials reference managers raising $10–20M funds and Start Fund targeting sub-$10MM first closes — globally distributed (VC Lab operates worldwide; Start Fund excludes Europe/Singapore/India/Japan jurisdictions).

## 8. Go-to-Market Motion (the interesting part)

Decile's GTM is a vertically integrated flywheel rather than classic enterprise sales:

```
Free content/training (VC Lab, articles, LinkedIn guides)
        ↓ (17K+ applicants over time)
Cohort enrollment (free) ──► Mensarius Oath / community norms
        ↓
Decile Hub adoption (free tier; default tool inside cohorts)
        ↓
Paid conversion: Hub upgrades + Start Fund launches
        ↓
Decile Partners (agentic fund admin) ← highest-value contract
        ↓
LP Institute / Decile Pro (LP side) → Decile Capital (FoF invests)
```

startuply.vc (Apr 21, 2026) describes this explicitly: Decile "converts free VC Lab trainees and SaaS users into agentic fund admin customers for Decile Partners," calling the built-in distribution channel "a clear advantage in a crowded market."

Supporting motions: heavy SEO/content operation (govclab.com insights cadence: multiple posts weekly through 2025–2026 covering fundraising schedules, AI tools, women in VC, next-gen GPs), responsive product development marketed publicly ("Over 900 firms tell us what they need, and we build it - quickly"), and PR moments (Axios coverage of Decile Base).

## 10. Competitive Positioning

**Direct competitors (emerging-manager fund OS/admin):** Carta (fund admin + equity giant), AngelList (fund infra; Caplight's closest comps also include Edda and Odin), plus fund-ops tools catalogued by the VC Stack directory: Totem VC, Vestberry, Visible.vc, Rundit, Vestlane, Ark, Valuat.
**CRM-only rivals they attack on price/scope:** Affinity (relationship intelligence; Decile claims it costs "thousands/month" and lacks LP/fund features), DealCloud (enterprise-heavy, slow implementations), Attio/Airtable/Notion DIY stacks.
**Adjacent threat surface:** Wokelo/Roulette (this report set) nibble at deal-flow/diligence edges of the stack; horizontal AI agents could commoditize back-office automation.

**Differentiators (as evidenced):**
1. Only player bundling training + software + admin + LP capital for this segment.
2. Freemium pricing floor competitors hesitate to match.
3. Proprietary knowledge base (Base) trained on years of manager Q&A.
4. Brand/community (Mensarius Oath, "Venture 2.0" narrative) resonating with new managers.
5. Distribution via cohort alumni network — a moat that compounds.

**Weaknesses visible in public record (analysis):**
- Revenue concentration risk: the model depends on the emerging-manager formation cycle; VC formation is cyclical and rate-sensitive.
- Self-reported metrics dominate the evidence base (NPS 94, zero churn, $1.5B commitments, 70% cost cuts) — impressive if accurate, but not independently audited; third-party writeups carefully attribute them as claims.
- Freemium SaaS + services + FoF is operationally complex (regulated activities: fund administration, tax prep) with margin heterogeneity.
- Related-party dynamics: Decile Capital investing in funds that pay Decile Partners creates potential conflicts LPs may probe during diligence.
- Geographic limits on Start Fund (no EU/Singapore/India/Japan) leave large manager geographies to competitors or traditional counsel.
- Brand dependence on a single high-profile founder.
