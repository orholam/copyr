## How They Collide

- **Decile ↔ Roulette:** Roulette attacks the deal-flow/CRM layer Decile bundles, undercutting with transparent per-seat pricing while conceding fund admin entirely. Complementary for many funds; competitive at the deal-flow module level.
- **Decile ↔ Wokelo:** Minimal direct overlap today (fund operations vs. diligence research), but both court emerging-manager buyers under "AI for VC" positioning; Geek Ventures appears on both cap tables.
- **Roulette ↔ Wokelo:** Adjacent lanes — pipeline management vs. research depth. Roulette's AI memos brush against light diligence; Wokelo's akta.pro could power tools like Roulette rather than compete head-on.

## Shared Observations Across All Three

1. All three sell to the same macro trend: AI-native tooling replacing manual analyst/GP work in private markets.
2. Evidence quality varies by company maturity: Wokelo has the strongest independent corroboration (KPMG press release, GeekWire, G2); Decile has scale but largely self-reported metrics; Roulette is almost entirely self-described.
3. Pricing strategies are strikingly divergent for adjacent products: free-core (Decile), disruptive flat-rate ($50, Roulette), opaque enterprise custom (Wokelo).
4. Each carries a single-point concentration risk: Decile (founder brand), Roulette (parent fund), Wokelo (KPMG relationship).
